A banner warns that a match's raw footage is about to be deleted. Raw footage is kept for 7 days and unfavourited clips for 14.

- **Provide:** the time left, the deletion date, and what happens at deletion.
- **Levels:** from 3 days down to 24 hours, it uses the warning style with `role="status"`. Under 24 hours, it switches to `wl-banner--danger` with `role="alert"`.
- **Placement:** at the top of the review workspace, on the match page, and inline in review queue rows as a compact clock-and-time label.
